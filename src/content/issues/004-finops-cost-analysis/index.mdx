This video demonstrates how CoCo helps you analyze Snowflake costs and find savings.

## What you will see

- Querying ACCOUNT_USAGE views for credit consumption
- Breaking down costs by warehouse, service type, and user
- Identifying idle warehouses and optimization opportunities

## Why it matters

Understanding your Snowflake spend is the first step to optimization. CoCo makes it easy to write the right queries against ACCOUNT_USAGE without memorizing view names or column structures.
